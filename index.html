About Me

I am an undergraduate Frontend Developer and UI/UX Designer with a strong focus on building clean, responsive, and user-centered web interfaces. I enjoy translating design concepts into functional, intuitive digital experiences using modern frontend technologies.

Currently pursuing my degree in Computer Science at Bahria University, I have gained hands-on experience through academic and personal projects that have strengthened both my development and design skills. Alongside frontend development, I have foundational exposure to backend concepts and am actively learning backend development, cybersecurity, and AI/ML to broaden my technical skill set and deepen my understanding of full-stack systems.

I am eager to grow as a developer, contribute to real-world projects, and continuously improve my skills at the intersection of design and development.
